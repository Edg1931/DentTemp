Frequently asked

Honest answers, no fluff.

Pricing, verification, payouts, migration — here's everything offices and pros tend to ask.

For dental offices

How is DenTemp different from an agency?
No placement fees, no markups on pro rates, and pros are matched directly with you rather than routed through a recruiter. You pay one flat monthly membership for unlimited hiring.
How quickly can I fill a same-day shift?
Average fill time across the platform is 47 minutes for posted same-day temp shifts. Scheduled shifts and permanent jobs typically attract qualified candidates within hours.
What if a temp doesn't show up?
Reliability scores flag risky profiles before you confirm. If a no-show happens, our team helps you re-fill the shift immediately, and the pro's standing is impacted significantly.
Can I bring my existing trusted temps over?
Yes — your onboarding specialist will help you invite them. They join free, and you can save them to your favorites list for one-tap re-booking.
Do you require a contract?
Membership is month-to-month. No long-term contract. Cancel anytime from your dashboard.
Will I be charged extra for permanent hires?
No. Whether you hire someone for one shift or as a permanent associate, your monthly membership is the only cost. We don't take a placement fee.
Will this require a migration from our current site or system?
DenTemp lives alongside your existing practice software — no migration of patient data or PMS data is needed. We'll help you bring over team contacts, saved temps, and any open positions.

For dental professionals

Is it really free for me?
Yes. Hygienists, assistants, and dentists join free. We take $0 from your pay. You set your rate; the office pays it.
How do I get paid?
Direct deposit within 48 hours of a completed, confirmed shift. Tax forms are handled in-platform — 1099 at year end for most temp work.
What documents do I need to upload?
Current license, BLS, X-ray cert (if applicable), and ID. Other certs (OSHA, EFDA, anesthesia) are optional but boost visibility.
Can I work in multiple states?
Yes — upload a license for each state. We'll surface shifts in any state where you're verified.
What does the two-way review system mean for me?
You review offices, and offices review you. You can see how an office treats temps before you accept. Great reviews make you more visible and more re-bookable.

Privacy, trust & access

How do you protect my data?
All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We follow HIPAA-aware practices for any document storage, and we never sell your information to anyone.
Is the site accessible (ADA / WCAG)?
Yes. DenTemp targets WCAG 2.2 AA: high color contrast, keyboard navigation, screen reader labels, focus indicators, and skip links throughout.
Will my existing users carry over from the current DenTemp site?
Yes — every existing DenTemp account will migrate to the new platform automatically. Profiles, documents, and reviews come with you. We'll prompt for a quick refresh on signup.
