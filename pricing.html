Pricing questions

The fine print, made simple.

Is there really no placement fee?
Correct. Whether you hire someone for a single shift or as a permanent associate, your membership is the only cost. We are not a recruiter and never take a percentage of salary.
What's included in the 14-day free trial?
Everything in the Practice plan. Post unlimited shifts and permanent jobs, see verified profiles, message pros, and confirm hires. No credit card required to start.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel any time from your dashboard. Your membership stays active through the end of the current billing month.
How do pros get paid?
Pros invoice the office at the agreed rate via DenTemp. Most pros receive direct-deposit payouts within 48 hours of a completed shift. Offices pay one consolidated invoice — never a per-placement fee.
Do you offer annual billing?
Yes — annual billing saves you two months. Contact us to switch.
