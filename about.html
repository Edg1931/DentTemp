Our story

Built by dental people. For dental people.

DenTemp started with a simple frustration: dental hiring is hard, expensive, and impersonal. We thought it could feel warmer, faster, and a whole lot fairer.

The dental workforce deserved better software.

For decades, dental offices have paid five-figure placement fees to fill chairs, and dental professionals have lost a piece of every paycheck to agencies. Meanwhile, the rest of the world started hiring through beautiful, transparent, two-sided platforms.

We're building DenTemp as the digital network for the dental workforce — combining hiring, availability, and a real community in one place. Offices get to keep more of what they earn. Pros set their own rates and build a profile that travels with them. And everyone gets reviewed, because trust should run both ways.

We believe that the people who care for our smiles deserve careers that feel good — and that the practices who hire them deserve tools that respect their time and their margin.

What we believe

The principles we build by.

Warm beats slick

We'd rather feel like a hand-written note than a high-pressure pitch. The dental community is small — we treat it that way.

Transparent or nothing

No hidden fees, no surprise invoices, no surge pricing during a snowstorm. Honest pricing always wins.

Both sides matter

A platform only works when offices and pros feel respected. Reviews go both ways. Voice goes both ways.
