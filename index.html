Common questions

The honest answers, up front.

How is DenTemp different from a traditional dental staffing agency?
Agencies typically charge offices a placement fee (often 15–25% of salary) and take a cut from temp pros. DenTemp is a flat-rate membership — you pay one predictable monthly fee and keep 100% of what you earn or save. Pros set their own rates, offices keep relationships, and nobody is squeezed in the middle.
Is it really free for dental professionals?
Yes. Hygienists, assistants, and dentists join, build a profile, and work shifts at no cost. You set your rate; we take no cut from your pay.
How fast can a temp shift get filled?
Most same-day shifts get a verified offer within 30–60 minutes. Scheduled temp shifts and permanent jobs typically attract qualified candidates within hours, not days.
How do you verify professionals?
We verify state license, certifications (BLS, X-ray, OSHA, etc.), employment history, and run an optional background check. Documents are stored in a HIPAA-aware encrypted vault and re-verified at renewal.
What happens if a temp doesn't show up?
You're protected. Our reliability score flags risky profiles, our team helps re-fill the shift fast, and no-shows hurt a pro's standing — strongly. We're investing in trust, not just tech.
Can I migrate from a different staffing platform?
Absolutely. We'll help you import your office profile, team list, and (with their permission) carry over existing pro relationships. Our onboarding team handles it in a single call.
