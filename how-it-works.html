How DenTemp works

Two flows. One delightful experience.

Whether you're staffing a chair or building a career, here's exactly what to expect.

1

Sign up & verify

Create your office profile, add your team, and confirm your practice details. Takes about 5 minutes.

2

Post a shift or job

Pick date(s), role, software, rate range, and any required certs. Save templates for next time.

3

Review verified offers

See pros, their rates, reviews, and credentials. Message before you commit.

4

Confirm with one tap

Auto-confirmations go out by SMS & email. We send a friendly reminder the day before.

5

Welcome them in

Day of: they arrive prepared. Software, address, and contact are already on their phone.

6

Review & rebook

Leave a quick rating. Save favorites. Rebook your best people with a tap next time.

1

Build your profile

Upload your license, certs, photo, and bio. We verify in the background, usually same-day.

2

Set your defaults

Default rate, preferred days, max travel distance, software you know. Edit anytime.

3

Browse open shifts

See matches on a map. Filter by role, rate, software, or office rating.

4

Send an offer

Offer at the rate you want — or just accept the posted one. Message the office first if you like.

5

Show up prepared

Your phone has the address, parking, software, and contact ready to go.

6

Get paid & reviewed

Direct deposit within 48 hours. Earn reviews. Save your favorite offices to rebook with.
